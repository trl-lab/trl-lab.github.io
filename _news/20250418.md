We're hiring two PhD students on LLM-powered Responsible Data Analysis and Fundamental Techniques for TRL!

---

Interested in research on Table Representation Learning (TRL) to democratize insights from structured data? Then you are a perfect candidate for <a href="https://trl-lab.github.io/open-positions/" target="blank">one of the two new PhD positions</a> we have opened in our lab. Both PhD students are advised by Dr. Madelon Hulsebos in collaboration with the University of Amsterdam where they will also get their PhD degree from. <br>

The <a href="https://trl-lab.github.io/people/" target="blank">TRL Lab</a> provides a vibrant research environment at CWI in Amsterdam for pioneering research on all aspects related to TRL. We have also initiated the <a href="https://ivi.fnwi.uva.nl/ellis/research/table-representation-learning/" target="blank">TRL Research Theme ELLIS unit Amsterdam</a>, where we host workshops, monthhly seminars, and biweekly reading groups. Come join us!
