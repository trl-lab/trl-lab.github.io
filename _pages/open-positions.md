### Research Engineer

Are you interested in contributing to the democratization of insights from tabular data by supporting new research contributions in tabular AI? Do you have strong skills in AI coding and infrastructure to ensure usability of AI research in practice? Are you passionate about bringing the development of new models, tools, systems, and interfaces to a mature level to scale-up promising research ideas? Are you eager to stay on top of the latest developments regarding AI tools and infra? Then you might be a great fit to join the TRL Lab as a research engineer! If you are interested, please send an email with your CV to Madelon Hulsebos <a href="mailto:madelon@cwi.nl" target="blank">madelon@cwi.nl</a>.
