### PhD and postdoc positions (funded)

<b>We are hiring new PhD students through the <a href="https://ellis.eu/phd-postdoc" target="blank">ELLIS PhD program</a> to conduct research on Table Representation Learning</b>. The positions are supervised by <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com" target="blank">Madelon Hulsebos</a>, and can be in the following directions 1) Fundamental Techniques for Table Representation Learning, 2) Reliable Mechanisms for AI-Powered Data Analysis, or 3) Models and Tools for Exploratory Data Analysis and Data Science (co-supervised with <a href="https://marinelm.github.io/" target="blank">Marine le Morvan</a>). <b>Applications are due 31 October. You can apply <u><b><a href="https://ellis.eu/news/ellis-phd-program-call-for-applications-2025" target="blank">here</a></b></u>!!

The PhD positions are based at CWI and will receive their degree from the University of Amsterdam. The ELLIS co-supervisor depends on the topic and can be discussed during the interview process. Both are fully funded 4-year positions with a gross monthly salary that increases from €3,059 in year 1 up to €3,881 in year 4. You will receive an extra holiday allowance (8%) and year-end bonus (8.233%), as well as 42 vacation days. More about the working conditions can be found <a href="https://www.cwi.nl/jobs/terms-of-employment" target="blank">here</a>.<br>

### Master thesis projects (2026)

This year, we host master thesis projects on the following topics:

<ul>
<li>Contextual agentic data science pipelines (supervised by <a href="https://effyli.github.io/" target="blank">Effy</a> & <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/" target="blank">Madelon</a>)</li>
<li>Failure modes in tabular data science agents (supervised by <a href="https://effyli.github.io/" target="blank">Effy</a> & <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/" target="blank">Madelon</a>)</li>
<li>Robust open-domain insight extraction from tabular data (supervised by <a href="https://www.daniel-gomm.com/" target="blank">Daniel</a> & <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/" target="blank">Madelon</a>)</li>
<li>Assessing generated insights from tabular data (supervised by <a href="https://cornelius-wolff.de/" target="blank">Cornelius</a> & <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/" target="blank">Madelon</a>)</li>
<li>New small-scale efficient text-to-SQL models (supervised by <a href="https://cornelius-wolff.de/" target="blank">Cornelius</a>/<a href="https://www.daniel-gomm.com/" target="blank">Daniel</a> & <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/" target="blank">Madelon</a>)</li>
<li>Synthetic tabular data generation (supervised by <a href="https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/" target="blank">Madelon</a>)</li>
</ul>

We typically work with students from the University of Amsterdam, who will get a desk at CWI and will receive a small monthly allowance. Applications from UvA students can ideally be sent through the UvA's thesis portal or to <a href="mailto:madelon@cwi.nl" target="blank">madelon@cwi.nl</a>. We may make exceptions for talented students from other European universities with an exceptionally good background fit for a project, then you can send an email to Madelon as well.<br>


### Temporary collaborations

We also host highly motivated senior PhD visitors from foreign institutes, and research master students from Dutch universities. Please send an email with your CV and interest statement <a href="mailto:madelon@cwi.nl" target="blank">madelon@cwi.nl</a>.

If you are an international ELLIS Honors bsc/msc student and want to visit us with support of the <a href="https://ivi.fnwi.uva.nl/ellis/funding/ellis-msc-hosting-programme/" target="blank">ELLIS MSc Hosting Program</a>, please reach out to Madelon to align on a plan.
