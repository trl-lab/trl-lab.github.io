Félix is a PhD Student in Machine Learning in the SODA team at Inria Saclay under the supervision of Gaël Varoquaux, since January 2023. His research focuses on developing methods to integrate external knowledge into tabular learning. Prior to his PhD, he completed a research internship within the same team, working on large-scale knowledge-graph embedding. Félix hold degrees from the MVA research master and Ecole Polytechnique.
